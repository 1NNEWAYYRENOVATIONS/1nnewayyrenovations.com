Who is 1NNEWAYY RENOVATIONS LLC?
1NNEWAYY RENOVATIONS LLC is a Florida renovation company serving residential and commercial customers. Our work is built around clear communication, dependable project coordination, quality workmanship, and completing work according to the agreed project scope.
What types of renovation projects do you handle?
Our services include flooring, drywall, painting, framing, and general renovation work. Flooring options include LVP, carpet, and metallic epoxy. We also coordinate electrical and plumbing work when a project requires those specialty trades.
What areas do you serve?
Our primary service area is Hillsborough County and the surrounding Tampa Bay market. Service availability can vary by project size, location, schedule, and scope. Contact us with the project address and details so we can determine whether the project is within our service area.
How do I start a renovation project?
Start by submitting a Request Onsite Estimate through the website or contacting our team directly. We review the project goals, property information, requested work, and other relevant details before determining the appropriate next step.
What happens during an onsite estimate?
The onsite estimate is used to understand the existing conditions, measure or inspect relevant areas, discuss the desired result, identify project requirements, and clarify the scope of work. This information helps establish a more accurate project proposal.
How is the project scope and pricing established?
Pricing is based on the agreed scope of work and the conditions, materials, labor, and specialty services applicable to the project. The objective is to establish clear expectations before work begins and to identify material changes to scope as the project progresses.
When is my project start day confirmed?
The project start date is confirmed once the project requirements have been established as well as submitted electronic estimate has been viewed, approved, and signed by customer; in addition, the required initial payment must clear to confirm scheduling and any project-specific conditions.
How are changes to the project handled?
If the requested work changes after the original scope is established, the change should be reviewed before the additional work proceeds. Scope, materials, scheduling, and pricing impacts should be clarified so both sides understand the effect of the change.
How do you handle electrical and plumbing work?
Electrical and plumbing requirements are evaluated as part of the project scope. When specialized electrical or plumbing work is required, 1NNEWAYY RENOVATIONS coordinates that work through appropriately licensed trade contractors rather than representing general renovation work as a substitute for specialty licensing.
Is 1NNEWAYY RENOVATIONS insured?
Yes. The company maintains general liability insurance with $1,000,000 in general liability coverage and $2,000,000 in aggregate coverage. Coverage should be evaluated against the requirements of a particular contract or project before work begins.
Do you have employees or use subcontractors?
The company operates with subcontracted project resources rather than full-time employees. Specialty trade work is coordinated with qualified trade contractors as required by the project. Project responsibilities and the applicable scope are addressed during project planning.
What payment methods do you accept?
Accepted payment methods include credit and debit cards, checks, and bank transfers. The applicable payment schedule and required initial payment are established according to the project agreement.
Do you offer financing?
Financing or payment-plan availability can depend on the project and any third-party financing arrangements in effect at the time. Ask about current options when requesting an estimate rather than assuming financing is available for every project.
How do you keep customers informed during a project?
Project communication is an important part of our process. Customers should receive clear information regarding scope, scheduling, material decisions, project changes, and other issues that may affect the work. The specific communication cadence can vary based on project size and complexity.
What should I prepare before requesting an estimate?
Be prepared to describe the areas you want renovated, the improvements you have in mind, your preferred materials or finishes if known, the property's location, and any known issues or special requirements. Photos can also help provide useful preliminary context.
How can I review completed work or submit a review?
Visit the Our Work and Reviews sections of the website to view available project information and customer feedback. Customers can also use the website review form or the available Google Review option to share their experience.
What if an issue is discovered during the project?
Existing conditions or unforeseen issues can sometimes become visible after work begins. When that happens, the issue should be documented, discussed, and evaluated for its effect on scope, materials, cost, and schedule before proceeding with materially different work.
How do I request an onsite estimate?
Use the Request Estimate tab in the website navigation to submit your project information. You can also use the Call Us or Email Us options to contact the company directly.
