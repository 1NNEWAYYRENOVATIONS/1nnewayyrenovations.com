LVP vs. Carpet: Which Flooring Is Better for Your Florida Home?

Compare LVP and carpet for Florida homes by room use, maintenance, comfort, appearance, and renovation goals before choosing a flooring direction.

LVP and carpet can both work well in a renovation, but they solve different needs. Instead of asking which material is universally better, start by asking what each room needs from the finished floor.

Where LVP can make sense

Luxury vinyl is often considered when the goal is a hard-surface appearance with straightforward day-to-day cleaning. It can be a strong fit for living areas, hallways, kitchens, and other spaces where the homeowner wants a continuous hard-floor look.

Where carpet can make sense

Carpet brings softness and a different feel underfoot. Bedrooms, stairs, and rooms where comfort and sound absorption are priorities can be good candidates for carpet, depending on the overall project.

Compare the project—not just the product

Consider the condition of the existing floor, room-by-room use, transitions, trim, furniture, maintenance expectations, and the look you want. The installation scope and preparation work can matter just as much as the material itself.

A simple room-by-room approach

  • Living areas: consider traffic, cleaning, and visual continuity.
  • Bedrooms: prioritize comfort, appearance, and the way the room is used.
  • Stairs: evaluate safety, existing construction, and the desired finished appearance.
  • Connected spaces: plan transitions before ordering materials.
